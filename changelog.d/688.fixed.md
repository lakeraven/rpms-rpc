`Coverage.add_private_insurance` with no end date reports the policy it filed (#688)

- Without `ends_on:` the gem left `AGGPIEED` and `AGGPHEED` out of the PARMS. `UPD^AGGUPPVT` filed the policy, then `UPOL^AGGUPPVT` read `AGGPHEED` bare (only PARS sets it) and died LVUNDEF before the result row. The gem reported `:coverage_empty_reply`, and a retry would file a second policy. Both end-date fields are now sent blank when no end date is given, so the build answers its result row and the gem returns the record it filed. A blank end date passes validation; any other end date must still be a real MM/DD/YYYY date.
- Proven on a throwaway container of bcer-9.0-20261109-d3adcff-ydb as PROFILE,REG (`test/live/coverage_private_no_end_date_live_test.rb`): one policy filed, reported with its record IEN, on file once with no end date. The `add_private` flow in `coverage.yml` is re-captured with the new frame.
