`CiaClient#call_rpc_global_array` returns a stock error reply at the broker's end of data instead of waiting 30 s for a `$C(31)`, and the AMHG reads raise its `M ERROR|` row (#706)

- A GLOBAL ARRAY reply whose routine failed carries no `$C(31)`: `ERR^AMHGU` writes `M ERROR|<text>` over row 1 and returns, so `ADML^AMHGDA` never writes its closing node. The read waited for that sentinel until its timeout and dropped the session. It now ends at the broker's EOD, and goes on to the sentinel only when bytes past the EOD have already arrived (an EOD embedded in the body, #254). A data reply with its sentinel reads as before.
- `BehavioralHealth` reads (`Reference`, `Intake`, `Groups`, `CaseManagement` and the visit reads) raise `RpmsRpc::Client::RpcMError` with the row's text when the frame carries an `M ERROR|` row, instead of parsing it as a record.
- Proven on IRIS for Health Community 2026.2 (bcer-9.0-20261109-d3adcff-iris) and on YottaDB (bcer-9.0-20261110-55d9e3e-ydb, with its region set to refuse null subscripts and as shipped): `AMHG GET ADMIN RECORDS` for a provider with an administrative record and no POV took 31 s and raised `RpcTimeoutError`; it now raises the `ADML+32^AMHGDA` error in 1 s, and the session stays up.
