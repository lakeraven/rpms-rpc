Registration's duplicate lookup runs over BMX; over CIA `register` needs `lookup_client:` (#609)

- Over CIA an M error inside `AGG LOOKUP PATIENTS` answers the same bytes as a genuine no-match (ERR^AGGPTLKP writes the `$C(31)` DONE writes; its only signal, `BMXSEC`, reaches a BMX client alone). A faulted lookup could read as "no candidates" and let a retry file a second chart.
- `Registration.register`, `register_via_agg` and `Patient.register` take `lookup_client:`, a `BmxClient` signed on as the same user. The duplicate lookup runs on it, and every other call stays on `RpmsRpc.client`. Over CIA with no `lookup_client:`, registration answers `:identity_unverifiable` and sends nothing. A `lookup_client:` that is not a signed-on `BmxClient` raises `ArgumentError`. One signed on as another DUZ is `:identity_unverifiable`. When `RpmsRpc.client` speaks BMX, the lookup runs on it as before.
- Attributes can still be given as a Hash or as keywords.
- Proven on a throwaway container of bcer-9.0-20261109-d3adcff-ydb: a no-match over BMX reads as no match, a match is a duplicate, and a faulted lookup fails closed (`test/live/registration_lookup_bmx_live_test.rb`). Live registration specs over CIA now need `BMX_PORT`.
