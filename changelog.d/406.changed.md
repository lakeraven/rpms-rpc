The conformance gate reads the build's one RPC-surface document, `<tag>-rpcs.json` (#406)

`conformance:pin` now pins the release's `<tag>-rpcs.json` (openrpms#732) beside the packages and contexts faces, and every gate reads it through `RpmsRpc::Conformance::BuildSurface`: the registered-names, callable and fixture gates, `RpmsRpc::Features`, `rake rpc:exclusions`, `rpc:coverage` and `rpc:api_coverage`.

- `BuildSurface.load(dir, tag)` reads `<tag>-rpcs.json` (and `<tag>-contexts_19_05.txt` when present). It refuses an empty, repeated or unsorted list of RPCs.
- Removed: the per-file readers of `broker_8994.txt`, `rpc_reach.txt` and `rpc_signatures.txt` (`BuildSurface.from_files`, `BuildSurface.parse_registry_line`, `Rpc#ien`, `BuildSurface#header`). The pin no longer commits those three files; `rpcs.json` is bound to them through its `inputs`, which must match the release's sidecar.
- Kept: `rake conformance:ingest DUMP=` still reads openrpms's flat #8994 dump, and `RpcCoverage.load_registry` still reads a names file, as openrpms `bin/rpms_rpc_live_score.rb` writes.
