Master-overbook checks retain rights beyond a membership page (lakeraven-ehr#603)

`SchedulingSetup.master_overbook?` traverses resource-user entries by unique
record number so a repeated resource pointer cannot hide a user's master right
at a page boundary. Resource/user matching and the required enabled flag remain
unchanged. Synthetic wire tests cover boundary membership and negative controls;
this is not live RPMS verification.
