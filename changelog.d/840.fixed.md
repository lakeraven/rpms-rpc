`RpmsRpc::Lab.specimen(dfn)` with no `from:` returns the patient's newest specimen, instead of nil (#840)

- With no `from:` and `direction: :older` (the default), `specimen` sends tomorrow's FileMan date as DATE1 to `ORWLRR INTERIMG`, as `Lab.results` does. It sent a blank DATE1, which SELECT^LR7OGM reads as inverse date 9999999; nothing is older than that, so the routine answered nothing and the method returned nil for a patient with lab data.
- A patient with no LAB DATA still returns nil. `direction: :newer` with no `from:` still sends a blank DATE1.
