`BehavioralHealth.save_pcc_visit` links a BH visit to PCC through AMHG CREATE/EDIT VISIT (#537)

- `RpmsRpc::BehavioralHealth.save_pcc_visit(record_ien:, visit_ien: nil)` wraps stock `AMHG CREATE/EDIT VISIT` (`PCC^AMHGPCC`). One actual, always two pieces: the BH record IEN and the PCC visit IEN, the second blank when `visit_ien` is omitted so the routine falls back to #9002011 `.16` (`AMHGPCC.m:17-19`). It runs `EN^AMHBHRU`. The reply is a GLOBAL ARRAY: header `T00030Result`, then one row. `0~<text>` is `SaveError`. The text is named only when it is one of the fixed literals in `PRECHECK`/`CHECKREC` (`AMHBHRU.m:102-105`, `23-41`); any other `0~` text stays out of the message. Either way nothing was linked. A blank success row is `SaveError`: no PCC visit is linked, and an existing link may have been removed (`AMHBHRU.m:48-50`). `M ERROR|...`, a missing header, more than one data row, a `Client::ConnectionError` (including a timeout), and a `Client::RpcMError` are `SaveError` and the outcome is unknown. A transport or M error keeps that exception as the cause. `RpcNotAvailableError` and `RpcRefusedError` propagate. No message carries a caller value or broker text. Success returns `{ visit_ien:, new_link: }`, not the IEN string.
- `record_ien` and a given `visit_ien` must match `/\A[1-9]\d*\z/` after `to_s`, before any RPC. Every call then reads #9002011 `.08` and `.16` (flags `I`) before `AMHG CREATE/EDIT VISIT`. When `visit_ien` is given, or `.16` is already non-blank, the same RPC reads #9000010 `.05` and `.11` (DELETE FLAG, flags `I`) for that visit and refuses a missing visit, a `.05` that differs from the record's `.08`, a `.11` that is truthy or was not returned, or a `visit_ien` that differs from a non-blank `.16`. Truthy is the M test at `AMHPCCL.m:49` (non-blank and not `"0"`). With `visit_ien` omitted and `.16` already set, the edit path rewrites that visit and deletes its V files before any read-back (`AMHPCCL.m:46-74`). `ADD1` stores the visit as `.16` with no patient check (`AMHPCCL.m:21,33`) and links a deleted visit the same way (`AMHPCCL.m:49`). Any pre-read of #9002011 that is not a clean `[Data]` reply is `SaveError` before `AMHG CREATE/EDIT VISIT`, with or without `visit_ien`. `DdrFileman` cannot tell a missing entry from another failure (`error: true` with no fields, a garbled non-`[Data]` body, or a raised `RpmsRpc::Error`), so a record that is not on file is refused and AMHG is not called. The message says the behavioral health record could not be read and names no IEN. A row that is an IEN is returned only after a read-back of #9002011 `.08` and `.16` and #9000010 `.05` and `.11` (flags `IE`) shows `.16` equal to that row, the visit's `.05` equal to the record's `.08`, and `.11` clear, and, when `visit_ien` was given, the row equal to it. A reply that differs from a non-blank pre-read `.16` is `SaveError` before that read-back: the outcome is unknown and neither IEN is named. The return is `{ visit_ien:, new_link: }`. `new_link` describes the `.16` transition observed across the call. It is true only when the pre-read `.16` was blank and the read-back `.16` equals the reply. `new_link: false` means the link existed before the call and does not confirm the PCC visit was edited: `EN^AMHBHRU` returns success without touching PCC when the site link is off, the record is locked, or `^AMHREC(R,11)` piece 10 is set (`AMHBHRU.m:43-45`, `AMHPCCL.m:8,12`), and also when the primary provider has no link (`AMHLE2.m:76-83`). `EN^AMHBHRU` reads the BH site parameters (`^AMHSITE(DUZ(2),0)` piece 12 and provider links at `^AMHSITE(DUZ(2),11)`) and `^APCCCTRL`, not any clinic setting. DDR reads run outside the `AMHGRPC` scope, in the caller's context. A session with no declared context is re-bound to `CIAV VUECENTRIC` after the AMHG call, including when that call raises. The exception in flight is the one captured from that call, not `$!`, so a caller already inside `rescue` still sees a failed re-bind: `SaveError` and an unknown outcome when the call returned, and the original `SaveError` when the call raised. `save_visit` re-binds the same way.
