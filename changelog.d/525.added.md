`BehavioralHealth.save_health_factors` files health factors on a visit (#525)

Screening and measurements are `BehavioralHealth::Wellness` (#540).

- `save_health_factors` adds or replaces rows on one visit through `AMHG SAVE HEALTH FACTORS` (`HF^AMHGSVF`). List items join with `*`. Each item is `name~level~quantity~comment`. The level, quantity, and comment of each listed factor replace the stored values, and a blank clears that field. Mode `:edit` deletes every stored factor of the visit that was not listed. Mode `:add` leaves unlisted factors in place.
- Before any AMHG call the list must be non-empty, at most 25 factors, and must not repeat a name. A name is non-blank, at most 40 bytes (`String#bytesize`, which is `$L` when `ydb_chset=M`), and contains no separator. The B subscript is the first 40 bytes of `.01`. Each distinct name is pre-read on file 9999999.64 (B index, fields `.01;.1;.13`): the reply must have data, no error, and no MORE, every entry must be shaped, and exactly one row whose name matches, whose entry type is `F`, and whose inactive flag is not `1`. Any other entry whose `.01` has the same first 40 UTF-8 bytes is refused, whatever its entry type or inactive flag. Level is blank or exactly `MINIMAL`, `MODERATE`, or `HEAVY/SEVERE`, with no case folding and no stripping. Quantity is blank or a canonical number from 0 through 99999 with at most two decimal places (`+X=X`: `"0"`, an integer with no leading zero, or a fraction with no trailing zero; below 1 there is no zero before the point, so `".5"` is accepted and `"0.5"` is refused, as are `"1.50"` and `"1.0"`). Comment is blank or 1 to 245 bytes. The visit's patient and signature are read the same way as `save_visit`: another patient or a signed visit is refused. A visit with more than 25 stored health factors cannot be confirmed (the save then raises `SaveError` saying the record may have been filed), and a name with more than 25 B-index prefix matches is refused.
- A `0~reason` reply, an empty reply, or any other IEN is `SaveError`: the outcome is unknown and the record may have been filed. After a reply that names the record, `DDR LISTER` on #9002011.08 must show the sent rows. Mode `:edit` must match that set exactly. Mode `:add` must contain the sent rows. A sent level that reads back blank is a mismatch. A row for another patient or record, an error, a MORE row, or an unparseable row is `SaveError`. Messages carry no comment text.
