`BehavioralHealth.save_screening`, `save_health_factors`, and `save_measurements` file the wellness tab (#525)

- `save_screening` edits one visit through `AMHG SAVE SCREENING` (`SCREEN^AMHGSVF`). The wire is always mode `E`: `SCREEN^AMHGEVF` ignores the data mode and files the record. Results are the external set-of-codes text; `SCI^AMHGT` turns that into the internal code. Piece 9, the wellness primary provider, is sent because the RPC parses it, and it is not filed.
- `save_health_factors` and `save_measurements` add or replace rows through `AMHG SAVE HEALTH FACTORS` and `AMHG SAVE MEASUREMENTS`. List items join with `*`. Fields inside an item join with `~`. A measurement item keeps the empty second piece (`name~~value~provider`). Health-factor level is the external set-of-codes text, sent unchanged.
- The calls are fail-closed. A blank record IEN is refused on edit and on add, before any RPC. `|`, `~`, `*`, `^`, and control characters are refused in free text. A `0~reason` reply, an empty reply, or any other IEN is `SaveError`: the outcome is unknown and the record may have been filed. Health factors and measurements are confirmed with `DDR LISTER` after the reply. Mode `E` must match exactly. More than 25 items is refused, because that page cannot show them.
