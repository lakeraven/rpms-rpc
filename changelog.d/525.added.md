`BehavioralHealth.save_health_factors` files health factors on a visit (#525)

Screening and measurements are `BehavioralHealth::Wellness` (#540).

- `save_health_factors` adds or replaces rows on one visit through `AMHG SAVE HEALTH FACTORS` (`HF^AMHGSVF`). List items join with `*`. Each item is `name~level~quantity~comment`. The level, quantity, and comment of each listed factor replace the stored values, and a blank clears that field. Mode `:edit` deletes every stored factor of the visit that was not listed. Mode `:add` leaves unlisted factors in place.
- Before any AMHG call the list must be non-empty, at most 25 factors, and must not repeat a name. A name is non-blank, at most 30 characters, and contains no separator. Each distinct name is pre-read on file 9999999.64 (B index, fields `.01;.1;.13`): the reply must have data, no error, and no MORE, and exactly one row whose name matches, whose entry type is `F`, and whose inactive flag is not `1`. Level is blank or exactly `MINIMAL`, `MODERATE`, or `HEAVY/SEVERE`, with no case folding and no stripping. Quantity is blank or a canonical number from 0 through 99999 with at most two decimal places (`+X=X`: no leading zero except `0`, no trailing `.`, no leading or trailing space, and no trailing zero after a decimal point, so `1.50` is refused). Comment is blank or 1 to 245 characters. The visit's patient and signature are read the same way as `save_visit`: another patient or a signed visit is refused.
- A `0~reason` reply, an empty reply, or any other IEN is `SaveError`: the outcome is unknown and the record may have been filed. After a reply that names the record, `DDR LISTER` on #9002011.08 must show the sent rows. Mode `:edit` must match that set exactly. Mode `:add` must contain the sent rows. A sent level that reads back blank is a mismatch. A row for another patient or record, an error, a MORE row, or an unparseable row is `SaveError`. Messages carry no comment text.
