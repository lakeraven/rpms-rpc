`EmergencyRoom.visit` reads an ER visit, and `Adt.movements` a patient's admit, transfer and discharge movements (#747)

- `RpmsRpc::EmergencyRoom.visit(dfn:, visit_ien:)` reads one ER VISIT (#9009080) through `DDR GETS ENTRY DATA` under `CIAV VUECENTRIC`: arrival time, PCC visit, visit type, mode of transport, presenting complaint, ESI, ED provider, triage nurse and time, disposition, departure time and transfer destination. Pointers come back as `{ ien:, name: }`, times as `Time`, the ESI as an Integer.
- It is `nil` when the build has no such visit or the visit belongs to another patient. An id that is not a positive integer raises `ArgumentError` before anything is sent; no reply, or a reply outside GETS^DIQ's grammar, raises `Client::RpcError`, so an error text never comes back as a visit.
- AMER registers no RPC, so the read uses the generic FileMan RPC. BEDD (the ED Dashboard) is not a read path on YottaDB: it registers no RPC and keeps its data in IRIS class globals (openrpms#687, openrpms#1254).
- The replay tests use replies captured on `bcer-9.0-20261119-f67498c-ydb` (`er-visit.yml`), and `er_visit_live_test.rb` files an ER visit through `DDR FILER` and reads it back as PROV123.
- `RpmsRpc::Adt.movements(dfn:)` reads every PATIENT MOVEMENT (#405) row for a patient through `DDR LISTER` on its patient index, oldest first: time, transaction (`:admission`, `:transfer`, `:discharge`, `:specialty_transfer`, or a lodger one) and its name, type, ward, room-bed, specialty, and `admission_ien` / `discharge_ien` linking the stay. A patient never admitted reads `[]`; a DFN that is not a positive integer sends nothing; no reply, a refusal or a cut-short list raises `Client::RpcError`.
- Its replay tests use replies captured on `bcer-9.0-20261119-f67498c-ydb` after a stay was made on the throwaway through stock `$$ADD^BDGAPI` (`adt-movements.yml`). On that API a bed switch within a ward makes no transfer movement; it changes the admission's room-bed. The live spec reads a never-admitted patient; a live read of a stay waits on a seeded one (openrpms#1263).
