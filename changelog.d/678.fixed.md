`Medication.find` sends ORQQPS DETAIL the patient and the medication id (#678)

- `RpmsRpc::Medication.find(dfn, id)` replaces `find(ien)`: `DETAIL(ORY,ORPT,ORMED)^ORQQPS` takes the patient, then the id `ORQQPS LIST` gives (`<IFN><kind>;<O|I>`, e.g. `1R;O`). The id alone left `ORMED` undefined and the build died in M (`DETAIL+2^ORQQPS`). The route is now `GET /patients/{dfn}/medications/{id}`. A DFN that is not a positive integer, or an id not of that shape, answers nil and sends nothing.
- Proven on a throwaway container of bcer-9.0-20261109-d3adcff-ydb as PROFILE,MD (`test/live/medication_detail_live_test.rb`, `medication-detail.yml`). No demo patient has a medication, so the build answers DETAIL's own "No detailed information found."; the detail lines of a real medication are not yet seen.
