`RpmsRpc::Coverage` writes Medicare and Medicaid coverage through the AGG routines (#548)

- `add_medicare`, `delete_medicare`, `delete_medicare_record`, `add_medicaid` and `delete_medicaid` file and remove Medicare and Medicaid coverage through `AGG UPDATE MEDICARE` and `AGG UPDATE MEDICAID` (`UPD^AGGUPMCR`, `UPD^AGGUPMCD`), with `for_patient` reading it back through `BQI PATIENT INSURANCE`.
- Every write is fail-closed. Parameters containing `=`, `OVERRIDE`, a caret, a control character or the FileMan delete sentinel `@` are refused, as is a nil date, before any RPC is sent. Delete guards read FileMan on the caller's own client.
- When a Medicaid eligibility row fails to file, the record IEN the header step returned is deleted and `rolled_back` says whether that worked. If the eligibility call or the rollback raises, the result still carries `record_ien` (`error: :eligibility_outcome_unknown`, or `rollback_error`), and an unknown outcome is never cleaned up blindly.
- Array replies from XWB/BMX clients are joined into record-separated text before parsing.
