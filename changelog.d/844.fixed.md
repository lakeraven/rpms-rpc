`RpmsRpc::Lab.results` reads a specimen released before 10 o'clock as `:final`, not `:pending` (#844)

- GRID^LR7OGMG writes the "Report Released Date/Time:" line with `$$FMTE^XLFDT(.., "5MPZ")`, which pads the month and day but not the hour (`11/19/2026 1:12 AM`), and writes the date alone when DATE REPORT COMPLETED (#63.04 .03) has no time (`11/07/2026`). The parse required two hour digits and a time, so those released specimens came back `:pending` with no `released_at`.
- `released_at` now reads every shape the routine writes: a one- or two-digit hour, AM or PM, or the date alone (midnight of that day). A blank line still reads as `:pending`.
- A released line in any other shape raises `Client::RpcError` instead of reading as pending.
