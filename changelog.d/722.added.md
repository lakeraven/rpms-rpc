`Referral.record_follow_up` files a date of service and a case note on an open referral through BMC UPDATE REFERRAL in 30 parameters, and `Referral.follow_up_state` reads them back (#722)
Not yet run live. UPDREFRL^BMCRPC2 has 36 formals with RSLT and YottaDB takes 32 actual arguments, so the full frame fails with %YDB-E-MAXACTARG. This sends REFIEN through CN (31 actuals); the five formals left off are `$G`-guarded or unread. The routine rewrites the record, so the INCLUDE flags (401-412) and the one SNOMED CT term are read first and resent, and a closed referral, one linked to an EHR referral (1303), and one with several SNOMED CT terms are refused before anything is written. `test/live/referral_follow_up_live_test.rb` (writes, restores) is the live proof.
