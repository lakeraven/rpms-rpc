`RpmsRpc::Coverage.delete_private_insurance` removes one private policy row through DDR FILER (#382)

- `delete_private_insurance(dfn, record_ien:)` deletes one #9000006.11 policy row for that patient. It reads `.01` with child-first IENS `"<record_ien>,<dfn>,"` on the caller's client first. A reply that does not answer, a FileMan error, or a blank `.01` is `:verification_failed` and nothing is written.
- The write is one `DDR FILER` EDIT, field `.01` set to `@`, under `CIAV VUECENTRIC`. `AGG UPDATE PRIVATE INSUR` with PROC D is not sent: `UPD^AGGUPPVT` sets `AGGUPD(FILE,IENS,.01)="@"` before `IENS` is set (`AGGUPPVT.m:36-39`). After the filer reports success, `DDR LISTER` of #9000006.11 with IENS `",<dfn>,"`, fields `.01`, and MAX `*` must be a positive answer (a data section, `error` false, no `MORE`) that does not list the record IEN. Still listed, a nil reply, a FileMan error, no data section, or a `MORE` continuation is `:verification_failed`. A FileMan error is never absence. A filer refusal is `:delete_failed`. Success is `{ success: true, record_ien: }`.
