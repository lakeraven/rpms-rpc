`RpmsRpc::BehavioralHealth.save_pov` files a visit's purpose of visit through AMHG SAVE POV (#538)

- `save_pov` replaces the purpose-of-visit rows on a BH visit, and in mode E its Axis IV rows, through `AMHG SAVE POV` (`POV^AMHGSVF`). Axis III, Axis V and the GAF scale type go on the same eight-piece call.
- The call is fail-closed. A missing record, a signed visit, a patient that does not match, or a problem or Axis IV pointer that is not on file is refused before any RPC. Success requires the DDR read-back: POV rows on #9002011.01 (fields `.01;.02;.03;.04E`, flags `IP`, another record's `.03` ignored), Axis IV on #9002011.06101 by IENS, and Axis III as field 5301's word-processing lines joined together.
