`RpmsRpc::BehavioralHealth.save_pov` files a visit's purpose of visit through AMHG SAVE POV (#538)

- `save_pov` replaces the purpose-of-visit rows on a BH visit, and in mode E its Axis IV rows, through `AMHG SAVE POV` (`POV^AMHGSVF`). Axis III, Axis V and the GAF scale type go on the same eight-piece call.
- The call is fail-closed. Input validation runs before any RPC. A missing record, a signed visit, a patient that does not match, or a problem or Axis IV pointer that is not on file is refused before `AMHG SAVE POV`. More than 25 diagnoses or Axis IV entries are refused, because the read-back cannot verify them. A non-empty value that is blank after strip is refused; only exactly `""` clears Axis V or GAF. A narrative must satisfy the #9999999.27 `.01` input transform: 2 to 160 bytes of printable ASCII (`0x20`–`0x7E`) and no leading punctuation. C1 controls (U+0080 through U+009F) are refused in every free-text value, on `save_visit` as well as `save_pov`.
- `Client::FrameTooLongError` from the AMHG call is `SaveError`, with that error kept as the cause, for both `save_pov` and `save_visit`. The message says nothing was sent: the frame is rejected before the RPC goes out.
- Success requires the DDR read-back: POV rows on #9002011.01 (fields `.01;.02;.03;.04E`, flags `IP`, another record's `.03` ignored), Axis IV on #9002011.06101 by IENS, and Axis III as field 5301's word-processing lines joined together. Axis V (`.14`) and GAF (`1115`) must come back as present rows. A cleared field is a present blank row; a missing row or an empty read-back is a miss.
- `DdrFileman.lister` reports `data`: true when the reply had a `[Data]` section, false when it did not. A reply with no `[Data]` section has no entries. `save_pov` treats a POV or Axis IV read-back with no `[Data]` section as a miss, so a clear is not verified by silence. A POV row that is not exactly the four requested pieces with a positive-integer problem, and an Axis IV row whose `.01` is not a non-blank positive integer, is a miss rather than a dropped row. `save_visit`'s provider read-back treats a missing `[Data]` section as a miss as well.
