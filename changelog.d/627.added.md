`Agg.update_legal_names` edits or deletes a patient's LEGAL NAME row (#627)

`Agg.update_legal_names` wraps `AGG UPDATE LEGAL NAMES` (`LEGL^AGGPTNAM`) for an existing file 9000033 row: it checks that the row's `.02` is the patient before the write, from a read that must be exactly `[Data]` and one comma-less IENS row (`9000033^RIEN^.02^DFN^`), sends exactly one of `AGGLNDTC`, `AGGLNMC` or `AGGLGDOC` per edit, and refuses values LEGL would file wrongly (any byte outside printable ASCII, `@`, `^`, surrounding spaces, out-of-range byte lengths, and dates outside 1800-2699). DFN, proc and RIEN (at most 15 digits) are sent as plain Strings of the validated bytes. A failure reply after the write is sent says `written: :unknown`. A blank-RIEN add is refused and `AGGLNPRF` is not accepted, both left for a later change.
